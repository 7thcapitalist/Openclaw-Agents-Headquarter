# Vision

## Why this exists

Running a company means holding hundreds of small decisions, contexts, and
follow-ups in one person's head. OpenClaw's bet is that most of that can be held
by a team of AI employees who share context, keep institutional memory, and only
bring the founder the decisions that are genuinely theirs. The software factory
is the first department: it ships software with operator control and a full
audit trail.

## The bet (1–2 years)

An autonomous company operating system where multiple AI employees collaborate —
each project feels like it has real employees who understand it and improve it,
while the founder's attention is spent only on direction and strategic calls.

## Who this is for

A founder or very small team running one or more early-stage products who wants
leverage without losing control or auditability.

## Non-goals

- Not a no-oversight autopilot: the founder keeps merge and high-risk authority.
- Not a replacement for GitHub as the durable software record.
- Not a general chatbot platform; it is an operations system with gates.
- The factory does not deploy to production or spend money autonomously.
