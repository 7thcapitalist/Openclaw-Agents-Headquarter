# Budget alerts

HQ can evaluate company, project, and agent cost policies over lifetime or UTC
calendar-month windows. Results disclose priced consumption, remaining budget,
unpriced events, and warning/exceeded state. Enforcement is explicitly
`alert-only`: evaluation cannot pause agents, cancel work, or override founder
approval. Hard stops remain a separate founder decision and fault-testing task.

Adapted from Paperclip's budget service at pinned commit
`6abeb67334348dcb6fde2d591a27ffc7efc7118d` under MIT.
